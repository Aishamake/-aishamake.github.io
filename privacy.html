Privacy Policy

Learn how CodeCraft Innovation handles information and respects the privacy of its visitors.

Last Updated: November 8, 2026
Our Commitment: We are committed to respecting visitors' privacy and being transparent about how information may be collected and used.

1. Introduction

Welcome to CodeCraft Innovation. This Privacy Policy explains how our website handles information when you visit or interact with our platform.

CodeCraft Innovation is a technology and learning platform that publishes educational content about programming, artificial intelligence, data science, digital tools and technology.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for particular data processing activities, we will request that consent separately.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the services enabled on our website, the following categories of information may be processed:

Information You Provide

  • Your name, if you contact us.
  • Your email address, if you send us a message.
  • Information included in messages or enquiries.
  • Feedback, suggestions or other information you voluntarily provide.

Technical Information

Our hosting provider or other technical services may process information such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and technical request information.
  • Date and time of website access.

The exact information collected depends on the hosting, analytics and other services actually used by the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through our website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions and enquiries.
  • Improving our website and educational content.
  • Maintaining website security and performance.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website, where analytics are enabled.
  • Managing advertising services, where applicable.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on a visitor's device. They can support website functionality, remember preferences and help measure website usage.

CodeCraft Innovation may use cookies or similar technologies through its own features or third-party services.

If analytics or advertising services are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to perform their stated functions.

Visitors can manage cookies through their browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Advertising Services

CodeCraft Innovation may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers. These providers may use technologies such as cookies, identifiers or similar tools to deliver advertisements, measure performance and help prevent advertising abuse.

We plan to use Monetag as an advertising monetization provider. If and when its advertising services are integrated, the provider may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

We will update this policy to reflect the actual advertising services used on the website.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including educational platforms, documentation websites, learning resources and technology services.

These external websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or website content.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

7. Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our website against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods may depend on the nature of the information, the services involved and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to certain forms of data processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us using the information on our Contact Us page.

10. Children's Privacy

CodeCraft Innovation is an educational technology website. Our content may be useful to students and learners of different ages.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features, services, advertising arrangements or legal obligations change.

Any updates will be published on this page. Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on CodeCraft Innovation, please contact us.
